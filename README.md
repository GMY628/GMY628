# Hi there, I'm Mingyu Ge 👋

**M.S. Student in Artificial Intelligence | Multimodal Learning & Computer Vision**

I'm currently pursuing an M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence at **Dalian Minzu University** (2024–2027). My research focuses on **multimodal image fusion** and **visual representation learning**, particularly how to capture shared information and complementary cues across different visual modalities.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Multimodal Image Fusion:** infrared–visible, multi-focus, and medical image fusion
- **Representation Learning:** shared–private feature decomposition and cross-modal interaction
- **Learning Paradigms:** self-supervised learning and relation-constrained supervision
- **Continuous Representations:** implicit neural representations and arbitrary-scale image processing
